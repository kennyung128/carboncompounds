Quality Assurance & Third-Party Testing

Testing laboratory: Janoshik Analytical

Our commitment to quality

At Carbon Compounds, we believe in transparency, accurate product information and verifiable laboratory results. We use Janoshik Analytical, an independent analytical testing laboratory, for identity, purity and quantity testing.

Our three testing categories

  • 1. Identity testing. Helps confirm whether a sample contains the expected compound, and helps identify potential mislabeling or substitution.
  • 2. Purity testing. Measures the proportion of the target compound relative to detected impurities, using the laboratory's analytical method. Results help assess the chemical purity of the tested sample.
  • 3. Quantity testing. Measures the amount of the target compound in a sample, which helps determine whether the measured quantity corresponds to the product's stated specifications.

Certificates of Analysis (COAs)

Janoshik Analytical provides testing reports documenting the results of the analyses performed. Available documentation may include compound identification, purity results, measured quantity, testing date, laboratory report identification, and batch or lot information when provided.

Certificates of Analysis are available upon request for tested products. Reports can be verified through Janoshik Analytical's official verification system. See COA Verification.

Important testing information

Testing results apply to the samples analyzed. They do not guarantee that every product in a batch has identical characteristics. Our standard testing covers identity, purity and quantity. It does not establish sterility, endotoxin levels, or suitability for injection or human consumption. Independent analytical testing does not constitute FDA approval.

Storage and handling

We recognize the importance of appropriate storage and handling. Storage requirements vary by product, formulation and manufacturer, and products should be handled according to verified manufacturer specifications.

COA Verification

Certificates of Analysis are available on request for tested products. Just contact us.

How to request a COA

  1. Text us at 712-545-0455.
  2. Tell us what you need: the product name, the size or product code (shown in your cart), and the batch number if you have it.
  3. We reply with the available documentation for your product and batch. Availability varies by product and batch.

What a report may include

  • Compound identification
  • Purity results
  • Measured quantity
  • Testing date
  • Laboratory report identification
  • Batch or lot information, when provided

Batch numbers

Batch or lot numbers link a product to its testing records. When a batch identifier is available, it appears on the product label or the accompanying documentation.

How to verify a report

  1. Match the product. The compound and batch on the report should correspond to the product and batch you received.
  2. Find the identifiers. Janoshik Analytical reports carry a task number and a unique verification key (also shown as a QR code).
  3. Check with the lab. Confirm the report through Janoshik Analytical's official verification system at public.janoshik.com rather than relying on a forwarded copy.
  4. Check the details. Testing date, lab name and results should match what we told you.

What a COA does and does not show

A report describes the sample that was tested. It does not guarantee that every unit in a batch is identical, and it is not proof of sterility, safety or regulatory approval. Independent analytical testing does not constitute FDA approval.
